The table

A kitchen that
cooks in every direction.

The grill does most of the talking. Fajitas arrive still spitting on the plate. Quail, beef ribs and shrimp come off the same fire, and the parillada for four lands on a board carrying two quail, eight shrimp, sausage, beef and chicken.

Away from the grill the menu goes long and quiet. Enchiladas under chile gravy, salsa española, salsa verde or mole. Whole fried tilapia with a side of toasted bread. Caldo de res in a bowl the size of the plate under it, and menudo on weekends. Guacamole is made to order, which is why the menu prices it at market.

Between eleven and three, Monday through Saturday, there is a $9.99 plate that changes with the day and comes with tea. Children twelve and under get a drink with their meal. Both are dine-in, and both are printed on the menu.
